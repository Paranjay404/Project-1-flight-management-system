Develop a console-based Flight Management System using Python to simplify the management of
flight information and passenger bookings. 
The system should allow administrators to add, search, view, and delete flight records stored in CSV files. 
Passengers should be able to book and cancel flight tickets through a menu-driven interface. 
The application must automatically maintain seat availability by updating the number of available seats after 
every booking or cancellation. It should generate reports such as the complete flight list, booking details, 
and passenger records for easy monitoring and management. 
The system should prevent bookings when no seats are available and display 
appropriate messages to the user.
